A mark for YNWA

There was no logo. This is one — built from the geometry the site already uses, so the identity and the interface are one system rather than two decisions that happen to sit next to each other.

01The mark

Three figures on the mashrabiya diamond

The mark is the repeating unit of a mashrabiya screen — the pierced lattice that shades a window in Gulf architecture, and the same geometry already drawn at low opacity behind the hero. Reading inward, its three figures are the three stages of the proposition.

On accent
  • OuterStartThe market, entered from outside. It is the only figure that touches the edge.
  • InnerOperateThe company, running inside it.
  • CentreGrowThe only axis-aligned figure in the mark. Everything else is on the diagonal; this one has settled. Squared away is the entire service in one shape — and it is the element that survives at 16px, which is why it is solid.

Drawn in currentColor, so it works in ink, in maroon, reversed on dark, and in a single-colour stamp on a document. No gradient, no second colour, nothing that fails on a photocopier — which matters for a firm whose output is paperwork.

02Scale

It has to survive the favicon

A mark that only works on a billboard is not a mark. Left to right: browser tab, dropdown, header, contact card, letterhead.

At 16px the outer diamond and the solid centre carry it and the middle ring closes up — that is the intended failure mode, not a defect. The favicon at src/app/icon.svg is drawn separately with slightly heavier strokes and a wider inner gap, because hinting at that size is unforgiving.

03Lockup

Mark and wordmark

The wordmark is set, not drawn — Newsreader at 500 with 0.16em tracking. Live text stays crisp at every size, inherits the theme, and needs no second asset. Wide tracking on a serif reads as an institution; tight tracking on a grotesque reads as a startup.

Clear space

Clear space on every side is one mark width. It is a single number, measured off the mark itself, so it scales automatically and nobody has to look it up.

Beside Arabic

YNWA
خدمات تأسيس الشركات في قطر

The lockup does not mirror and the name is not transliterated. A logo is a fixed object — it sits at the inline-start edge in English and the inline-end edge in Arabic, but its own internal order never flips. And rendering "YNWA" in Arabic script would read as a foreign company localised badly, which is the opposite of the goal. If the firm has an established Arabic name, it sits beside this mark; it does not replace the wordmark.

04Alternates

Two directions I drew and set aside

Both are viable. If either reads better to you or to the client, swapping is a ten-line change in one file — src/components/ui/Logo.tsx — and nothing else in the codebase moves.

Selected

Three figures

Says the most with the fewest strokes, shares its geometry with the site's lattice, and holds at 16px. Chosen.

Set aside

Y in the aperture

A Y drawn only on the lattice's 45° angles, framed by the diamond. More literally the initial, and more memorable at large sizes — but a Y is a fork, and divergence is the wrong idea for a firm whose whole promise is a single clear route. It also crowds badly below 24px.

Set aside

Registration marks

Corner marks framing a solid diamond — the crop marks on a document, which is literally what this firm produces. The most distinctive of the three and the most modern. Set aside because the corners read as an unfinished bounding box at small sizes, and a mark that looks like a rendering artifact is a liability on a trust-driven site.

05Usage

Rules worth keeping

  • DoUse the accent maroon on light grounds and reversed on dark.The mark carries the one national reference in the system. It should be the maroon whenever it can be.
  • DoUse the mark alone once the wordmark is already on screen.Favicon, app icon, social avatar, a stamp in the corner of a document.
  • Don'tRotate it.The diagonal figures and the axis-aligned centre are the entire idea. Rotating collapses the distinction.
  • Don'tAdd a second colour, a gradient, or a shadow.The system has one shadow token and it belongs to the sticky header.
  • Don'tRe-set the wordmark in another face, or change its tracking.0.16em on Newsreader 500 is the wordmark. Plex Sans is not.
  • Don'tPlace it on a photograph without a solid plate behind it.The strokes are 1.5 units at a 32-unit box; they disappear over detail.

Still open

The name itself. The old site writes Ynwa, YNWA and Ynwas; the domain is ynwas.com; Instagram is @yanwaservices. Everything here is set in YNWA, which is the form the site used most and the one that works as a wordmark. If the client wants a different form — or if YNWA is an acronym that should be expanded somewhere — the wordmark changes and the mark does not.
